# MediShare User Guide

## Signing in
Use **Sign in with Google**. First-time users choose an account type. Individuals can start immediately;
organization, collection-centre and reviewer accounts stay **restricted** until an administrator reviews them.

## Individuals (households)
1. **Register Medicine** – optionally upload a photo of the pack and press *Read label*. If AI is enabled you
   can choose whether the photo may be sent to the AI provider. Always check every suggested field.
2. Restricted products (controlled, narcotic, psychotropic, cold-chain) cannot be registered – ask your pharmacist.
3. **My Requests → New request** – choose medicines, an authorized centre and pickup or drop-off.
4. Follow progress in **My Registrations** (collection & disposal trail) and **Notifications**.
5. You can cancel a request until the centre collects the medicines.

## Institutional donors (hospitals, pharmacies)
1. **Organization Dashboard** – register the organization, upload the registration certificate, add licences
   (e.g. hospital pharmacy licence) and **submit for verification**.
2. After verification, **Inventory → Add Inventory** (draft) → **Documentation** (attach purchase/source and
   storage records) → **Submit** from the Inventory tab. The system lists anything blocking submission.
3. Track decisions in **Review History**. *Approved for further authorized process* is not permission to transfer.
4. **Reports** exports your inventory as CSV.

## NGOs / healthcare organizations
1. Verify your organization (as above) – including a licence if you will receive medicines.
2. **Institutional Requests → New Requirement** → **Recommendations** → *Express interest*.
3. A reviewer assesses your interest; any transfer then needs recorded regulatory, quality and professional
   authorizations. Records appear under **Transfers & Reports**.

## Collection centres
1. Verify your organization and its bio-medical waste / collection authorization.
2. **Centre Portal → Centre Settings** – register each centre (hours, categories, capacity, location).
3. After admin verification: **Incoming Requests** (accept/reject) → **Collection Schedule** (schedule, then
   confirm receipt with actual quantities) → **Received Medicines** (initiate disposal) → **Disposal Records**
   (method, facility, manifest number, certificate) → *Close request*.

## Reviewers / pharmacists
Your credentials are verified by an administrator first. In the **Reviewer Portal**: *Start review*, inspect
product/batch/storage data and documents, complete the checklist and record a decision (approve for further
process, reject, hold, quarantine). Review expressions of interest and record **quality** and **professional**
transfer authorizations you have personally verified.

## Administrators
* **Admin Dashboard** – live KPIs, verification queue, delayed requests, alerts.
* **Users** – search/filter, suspend/reactivate (with reason), verify reviewer credentials, manage roles.
* **Organizations & Centres** – identity verification, licence verification, centre authorization.
* **Records & Reviews** – all registrations (hold/release/reject), inventory, requests, disposals, reviews,
  institutional requests, **transfer documentation** (regulatory authorization, mark authorized/completed).
* **Medicine Master**, **AI Risk Monitoring** (run assessment, acknowledge alerts), **Analytics & AI Assistant**,
  **Notifications** (broadcast), **Audit Logs**.
* **Super administrators** additionally manage **System Settings** (risk weights/thresholds, enabled organization
  types, email flag), role permissions, administrators and integration status.
